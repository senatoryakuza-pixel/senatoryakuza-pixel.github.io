Privacy Policy

App: Alireza Toronto · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Alireza Toronto ("the App") is a private tool used only by the creator Alireza (TikTok: @alireza.toronto) to manage his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the App may access basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), the list of the owner's own videos and their public statistics, and the permissions needed to upload and publish the owner's own videos.

How information is used

This information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to show performance statistics for the owner's account. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by the integration service used to connect to TikTok and are used only for the purposes above. Data is kept only as long as needed to provide these functions.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops all data access by the App.

Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
